# Think Before You Prompt

A responsive digital learning website for Psychology students about effective, responsible, and critical use of Artificial Intelligence.

## Files
- index.html — complete website (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in one file)

## How to use
1. Open `index.html` in a browser.
2. To publish with GitHub Pages, upload `index.html` to your repository and enable Pages from the repository's Settings > Pages.
3. The site is responsive and works on desktop and mobile screens.

## Suggested research output title
Think Before You Prompt: A Digital Learning Guide for the Effective and Responsible Use of Artificial Intelligence Among Psychology Students.

## Content basis
The guide incorporates human-centred AI education principles, including human agency, ethics, critical judgment, privacy, and meaningful use. UNESCO's AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) emphasizes human-centred mindset, ethics, AI techniques and applications, and AI system design, while its GenAI education guidance emphasizes human agency, ethical use, validation, and development of learner AI competencies.
