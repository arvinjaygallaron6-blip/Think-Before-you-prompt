Responsible AI Use

AI can support learning, but students remain responsible for understanding, checking, and using the information they receive.

💡
Learn

Use AI as Support

Use AI to brainstorm, clarify concepts, create practice questions, or explore ideas—not simply replace your own work.

🔎
Check

Verify Information

AI can produce incorrect or incomplete information. Check important claims against credible academic sources.

🧠
Think

Keep Your Judgment

Consider evidence, reasoning, limitations, and context before accepting an AI-generated answer.

⚖️
Act

Use AI Responsibly

Follow instructor policies, protect personal information, and maintain academic integrity.

Key idea: AI can provide information, but you are responsible for deciding whether it is accurate, relevant, and appropriate to use.

The THINK Framework

A five-step reminder for evaluating AI-generated information.

T

Test the Claim

What exactly is being claimed?

H

Hunt for Evidence

What evidence supports it? Can I verify it?

I

Inspect the Reasoning

Does the reasoning support the conclusion?

N

Notice Limitations

What could be missing, biased, uncertain, or oversimplified?

K

Know Before You Use

Do I understand it well enough to use it responsibly?

Critical Thinking Learning Materials

Four learning areas corresponding to the critical-thinking skills measured in the study.

🔎
01 • Analysis

Break Information Apart

Identify claims, assumptions, relevant details, and relationships.

Ask: What are the parts of this argument?

⚖️
02 • Evaluation

Judge the Evidence

Examine credibility, relevance, evidence, and reasoning.

Ask: How strong is the evidence?

🧭
03 • Decision-Making

Choose With Good Reason

Compare possible actions, consider consequences, and select the option best supported by evidence and academic responsibilities.

Ask: Which choice is most responsible, and why?

🧩
04 • Problem-Solving

Work Toward a Solution

Define the problem, break it into manageable parts, and compare practical solutions.

Ask: What steps could solve this problem?

Digital Learning Materials

Quick materials students can use before, during, or after using AI.

Material 01

AI Verification Guide

  • Identify the important claim.
  • Find a credible source.
  • Compare it with the AI response.
  • Check whether evidence supports the claim.
Material 02

Prompt With Purpose

  • Know what you want to learn.
  • Ask clear questions.
  • Request explanations, not only answers.
  • Challenge the response with follow-up questions.
Material 03

AI Red Flags

  • Unverifiable citations
  • Overconfident statements
  • Missing context
  • Claims without evidence
Material 04

Academic Responsibility

  • Follow course and school policies.
  • Do not misrepresent AI-generated work where prohibited.
  • Protect private information.
  • Understand what you submit.
