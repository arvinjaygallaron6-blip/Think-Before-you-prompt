Think Before You Prompt: 5-Step Guide

Pause before you accept an AI response. Your judgment stays in the process.

1

Question

What do I need help with?

2

Check

Is the AI information accurate?

3

Think

Do I understand the answer?

4

Evaluate

Can reliable sources support it?

5

Decide

How should I use the AI output?

Before Using AI, Ask Yourself:

  • ✓Did I understand the information?
  • ✓Did I verify the information?
  • ✓Did I use reliable sources?
  • ✓Did I make my own judgment?
  • ✓Did I use AI responsibly?

Remember

AI can assist your learning, but it should not replace your reasoning.

Use AI to explore possibilities, ask questions, and improve understanding. Then pause, evaluate the response, and make your own informed decision.

For Psychology students: Be especially careful with personal, confidential, sensitive, or identifiable information. Do not enter private case details or other protected information into AI tools.

Understanding AI in Psychology

AI can support academic tasks when used as a learning aid and with appropriate human oversight.

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence refers to computer systems that can perform tasks associated with human intelligence, such as generating text, recognizing patterns, organizing information, and responding to questions.

Where can AI help?

  • Explain difficult concepts in simpler terms
  • Brainstorm research ideas
  • Organize notes and ideas
  • Review writing and grammar
  • Generate practice questions
  • Offer alternative perspectives to examine

Use AI Effectively

The goal is to make AI support your learning—not do the thinking for you.

Better prompting

  • State your goal clearly.
  • Give relevant context.
  • Ask for explanations, examples, or questions.
  • Request different viewpoints when useful.
  • Refine your prompt when the response is unclear.

Good learning uses

  • “Explain this concept in simple terms.”
  • “Give me questions so I can test my understanding.”
  • “Point out weaknesses in my reasoning.”
  • “Give me possible perspectives to compare.”
  • “Help me improve this outline without writing the whole paper.”

Use AI Responsibly

Responsible AI use keeps people, learning, accuracy, and ethical judgment at the center.

🔍

Verify

AI can produce inaccurate or misleading information. Check important claims against credible sources.

🔐

Protect Privacy

Do not share confidential case information, identifying details, passwords, or other sensitive personal data.

✍️

Be Honest

Follow your school or instructor’s rules and clearly acknowledge AI assistance when required.

⚖️

Keep Human Judgment

Do not treat an AI response as automatically correct. You remain responsible for what you accept and use.

🌱

Keep Learning

Make room for independent reading, reasoning, discussion, writing, and problem-solving.

🤝

Use AI for Support

Use AI to assist your process while keeping your own ideas, decisions, and academic responsibility.

AI and Critical Thinking

Critical thinking means actively examining information before accepting it.

Practice analytical reasoning

Break complex information into parts, identify connections, and ask whether the explanation makes sense.

Evaluate information

Check evidence, source quality, assumptions, possible bias, and whether the AI response actually answers the question.

Strengthen problem-solving

Try to develop your own possible solution first, then use AI to compare approaches or identify gaps.

Make your own judgment

After considering the evidence and alternatives, decide what you believe is best supported and explain why.

Key idea: AI should be a thinking partner or learning aid—not the final decision-maker.

Resources & Quick Reference

Use these principles as a starting point for responsible AI use in education.

Core principles

  • Human agency: Keep human judgment and responsibility central.
  • Ethics: Consider fairness, privacy, safety, and academic integrity.
  • Critical judgment: Question and evaluate AI-generated information.
  • Meaningful use: Use AI when it genuinely supports the learning goal.
  • Continuous learning: AI tools change, so keep learning how to use them appropriately.

Reference: UNESCO, AI Competency Framework for Students (2024) and Guidance for Generative AI in Education and Research (2023).
